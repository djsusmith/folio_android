100% Offline & Open Source

Folio for Android

A calm, fast PDF reader, editor, converter, and signer. Built for Android with offline English, Bangla, and Arabic OCR — your files never leave your device.

Download Signed APK View on GitHub

Smallpdf-Style Tools Hub

Merge PDFs, split or extract pages, compress file size, convert Images to PDF, export PDF pages to PNG/JPEG ZIP, and convert DOCX to PDF offline.

Multilingual Offline OCR

Pre-bundled Tesseract models for English, Bangla (বাংলা), and Arabic (العربية) — search and copy text from scanned PDFs without internet.

Touch & Camera Signatures

Draw your signature directly with finger or stylus, capture from paper with your camera, or upload an image and place it on any page.

Edit, Annotate & Organize

Highlight text, add notes, draw freehand, insert text boxes, reorder or delete pages, and share exported PDFs via Android Share Sheet.
